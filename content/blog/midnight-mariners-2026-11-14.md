Keratoconus rejoins the Friday Midnight Mariners for the first time in a while after two PCs and a large portion of hirelings went missing after the previous session. We found ourselves in an as-far-as-I-can-tell unnamed ringfort in Hwicce. In today's session, there was Keratoconus, Arita's Cispia (can read and write Latin) and Jon's new character, Ollie (an archer). 

Last time I played with this group, Keratoconus had boasted that he would kill the giant Thrasir without a weapon, which gave some direction but we still fucked around for about 10 mins at the start of the session, determining that we must have already slaughtered our cow, Luke, before deciding that Old Bessie was next. Robin, your days are numbered. 

Looking out from the fort, we could see that there was a swamp with seven silver crosses in it to our West and after speaking to the locals, we learned that these were barrows and that there was some sort of local superstition about the dead rising from them. Initially, Keratoconus was eager to go graverobbing, since I've been hankering for some dungeoncrawling in this game, but he was dissuaded when the villagers informed us that they weren't structural barrows so much as piles of soil. 

So, we decided that without better leads, we'd head for the location of Thrasir's lair, which had been discovered by the group in the interim while I had been away. Along the way, we passed through a new area and found what appeared to be the scorched ruins of a fort, with blackened stones strewn all about. We investigated a bit and Keratoconus studied the scorch marks to ensure there wasn't a clear pattern to them. He did not want to get caught somewhere a dragon had raided before. We endeavoured to begin reconstructing the fort and set up camp for the night. What's the worst that could happen?

Cispia was collecting kindling with a torch lit from our campfire when she saw movement in the near distance, it looked to be a spectral figure. A ghost of a woman. Immediately, the woman began to rush towards her, Cispia called for help and rushed back to camp, where the ghost became more enraged. None of us spoke her language but the message was clear: She was begging for our help.

Hoping she was attracted to the flame and extinguishing it would sate her, Keratoconus grabbed Cispia's torch and threw it away from the camp, stamping out the firepit as well. At this point, it remained unclear whether this woman was dangerous or not, but we were all acting on the assumption that she was. This seemed to calm her a little and we began to regroup some.

At this point, it was Keratoconus' turn to notice something fucked up and he heard a slithering and crackling behind him. Crawling out of the remains of a chimney — which we hadn't noticed until this point — was some sort of lizard creature made from bone, which wasn't familiar to him at all. He froze, watching it carefully, polearm between the beast and himself. Keratoconus tried to quietly call the creature to the attention of the rest of the group but was unsuccessful and it lunged at him.

He ran to the firepit again, grabbing his spade and shovelling dirt onto the embers, hoping again in vain that if there were no flames, the creature wouldn't attack. The salamander pounced, coiling itself around Keratoconus and scorching his skin. Ollie boasted that he'd kill the creature with one well-placed arrow and we all entered into combat. 

I rolled poorly on initiative, which was a slight problem since after the deaths of fellow mariners, Keratoconus was the most senior commander in attendance and our hirelings acted on his turn. We each took a round of attacks, though without any close quarters weapons or even a polearm in hand, Keratoconus wasn't much use. Lucky rolls all round prevented attacks from damaging him while grappled.

Keratoconus tried a different tactic, attempting a stop, drop, and roll manoeuvre to smother the salamander. A third of his health was taken out in the struggle but it seemed like the beast was weakening. In the next round, we had good initiative rolls and both Keratoconus and Ollie could take action before the salamander, alongside the marines. Keratoconus gripped the ribcage of the monster, succeeding at a difficult feat of strength to pry them apart. The marines began to wail on the creature, each taking pot shots in its vulnerable state and then stepping aside for Ollie's arrow to strike true as the final blow.

We took a moment to regroup and then debriefed. This was Jon's first successful boast after what must be at least a dozen sessions of the campaign, so it was a pretty hype moment. The combination of the spirit who wanted fires to be extinguished and the salamander as a random encounter led to some really fun dynamics where it wasn't clear what was connected to what and made things super chaotic. We ran a bit overtime but it was chill, I wish I could make this game more often. Hopefully soon Keratoconus'll be back to kill Thrasir, I've got some ideas about how best to do it without armament....
